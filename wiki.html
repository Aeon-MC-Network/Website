Jobs Reborn System

Choose from 8 calibrated jobs (Miner, Hunter, Weaponsmith, Brewer, Woodcutter, Digger, Builder, Enchanter). Income payouts scale dynamically with AuraSkills levels.

Commands: /jobs join [job] • /jobs stats

Hand-Crafted Dungeons

Battle through 5 dungeon tiers featuring custom mythic mob boss fights. Chest loot is instanced per-player via Lootr so everyone receives unique rewards.

Commands: /dungeons • /warp Dungeons

Economy & Land Claims

Protect your builds using GriefPrevention claim blocks (earned per hour & via voting). Trade items safely using the Auction House and player warp shops.

Commands: /ah • /claim • /trust [player]
